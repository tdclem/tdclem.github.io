## Luhilde the Harvester, Now on DMs Guild!
It's been a while! I was sorting through my homebrew for Curse of Strahd when I found some notes I liked for a mini boss I ran early on in the campaign. I am excited to say I have released this homebrew boss monster as a new pay what you want supplement for Curse of Strahd on the DMs Guild, [Luhilde The Harvester!](https://www.dmsguild.com/en/product/549366/luhilde-the-harvester){:target="_blank"} The supplement aims to provide an interesting monster to use against a third level party of players, as well as some suggested hooks to use to introduce the monster in your game. I hope you enjoy running it!
